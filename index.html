ABOUT HAPPY & HEALTHY

Compassionate care for every stage of childhood.

Happy & Healthy was established in 2026 with a focus on supporting the mental wellbeing of children, adolescents and families through individualised psychological care.

Our work is grounded in evidence-based practice, with a warm and inclusive approach that recognises every child and family is different.
